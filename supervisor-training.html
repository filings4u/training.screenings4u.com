DOT agency coverage

Know which operating rule applies to your workforce.

49 CFR Part 40 provides common DOT drug and alcohol testing procedures. Covered employers also operate under their modal agency rule.

FAA

Aviation. 14 CFR Part 120

Drug and alcohol program requirements for covered aviation employers and safety-sensitive employees.

FMCSA

Motor Carrier. 49 CFR Part 382

Drug and alcohol testing requirements for CDL drivers and regulated motor carriers.

FRA

Railroad. 49 CFR Part 219

Drug and alcohol requirements for regulated railroad employees and employers.

FTA

Public Transit. 49 CFR Part 655

Drug and alcohol program requirements for covered public transportation employers and employees.

PHMSA

Pipeline. 49 CFR Part 199

Drug and alcohol requirements for pipeline operators and covered employees.

USCG

Maritime. 46 CFR Parts 4 & 16

Maritime drug and alcohol program requirements for covered vessel operations and personnel.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common supervisor training questions.

Learn about reasonable suspicion training, supervisor responsibilities, enrollment, and course completion.

Who needs DOT reasonable suspicion supervisor training?

Supervisors responsible for making reasonable suspicion testing determinations must complete the training required by their applicable DOT agency rule. Employers should identify which managers have this responsibility and apply the requirements for their operation.

What does reasonable suspicion training cover?

The course covers observable physical, behavioral, speech, and performance indicators associated with possible drug or alcohol misuse, along with appropriate observation, documentation, and referral procedures.

How many hours of supervisor training are required?

Requirements differ by DOT agency. For example, FMCSA rules require at least 60 minutes on alcohol misuse and 60 minutes on controlled substance use for supervisors who make reasonable suspicion determinations. Employers must confirm the training applicable to their own operating rule.

Can a supervisor order a test based on a suspicion alone?

Reasonable suspicion decisions must be based on specific, contemporaneous, articulable observations and the standards in the applicable DOT rule. Supervisors should document observations and follow their employer's testing procedures rather than relying on a personal assumption.

Does this course cover both drug and alcohol indicators?

Yes. The supervisor curriculum addresses indicators of possible controlled substance use and alcohol misuse, as well as the procedures for responding to workplace observations.

Can we enroll several supervisors at once?

Yes. Choose an individual or multi-supervisor training plan on this page. Organizations can also review the Group Training page for enrollment options.

Will supervisors receive training documentation?

Completion documentation is provided in accordance with the selected training plan and its completion requirements. Employers should retain the relevant training records for compliance purposes.

How do I access the course after enrolling?

Use the Student Login to open assigned training. Visit the Course Extensions page if additional access is needed.
