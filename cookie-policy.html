Cookie Policy

Workforce Training by screenings4u

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small data files stored by your browser. Similar technologies may include local storage, session storage, and tokens used to keep you signed in and maintain secure checkout or course sessions.

2. How We Use Them

  • Keep authenticated learners signed in.
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized use.
  • Remember necessary session and checkout information.
  • Support payment, video, and other platform functionality.
  • Understand technical performance and troubleshoot errors.

3. Essential Cookies

Some cookies or storage technologies are necessary for authentication, security, course delivery, and checkout. Disabling them may prevent portions of the Learning Center from functioning correctly.

4. Third-Party Technologies

Service providers such as our payment, hosting, authentication, or video-delivery providers may set their own cookies or similar technologies when their services are used.

5. Browser Controls

You can use your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Doing so may sign you out or interfere with Learning Center features.

6. Contact

Questions may be sent to support@screenings4u.com.
