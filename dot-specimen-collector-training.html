Course curriculum

Built around the collection process collectors perform.

Course content is structured around current U.S. Department of Transportation rules, agency-specific requirements, and practical workplace responsibilities.

01

Part 40 foundations

Collector role, prohibited conduct, privacy, integrity, and the relationship between Part 40 and agency rules.

02

Collection site security

Preparing the site, preventing access to adulterants, privacy controls, and required supplies.

03

Federal CCF

Step-by-step completion, signatures, copy distribution, corrections, and documentation.

04

Urine collection procedure

Specimen provision, temperature checks, split specimens, sealing, packaging, and shipment.

05

Problem collections

Shy bladder, insufficient specimen, temperature outside range, direct observation, refusals, and flaws.

06

Mock collections

Five consecutive error-free proficiency demonstrations covering routine and problem collection scenarios.

Proficiency demonstrations

Complete five mock collections as part of your qualification.

The mock collection work should demonstrate that the learner can perform the procedure correctly across routine and problem scenarios.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common collector training questions.

Find information about qualification, proficiency demonstrations, enrollment, and course access.

What do I need to qualify as a DOT urine specimen collector?

Under 49 CFR 40.33, collectors must meet the basic information requirements, complete qualification training, and successfully complete five consecutive error-free mock collections. Your training and proficiency demonstrations must be documented.

Are five mock collections required?

Yes. The DOT qualification standard requires five consecutive error-free mock collections covering specified scenarios. Completing online lessons alone does not replace these proficiency demonstrations.

Can a new collector take this course?

Yes. The training is designed for new collectors learning DOT urine specimen collection procedures and for existing collectors who need refresher instruction or retraining.

How long can I access the course?

Core collector plans provide 60 days of learner access. Check the selected training plan for its exact access period and included materials.

Does completing the online course mean I am qualified?

Not by itself. DOT collector qualification requires successful training and the required proficiency demonstrations. Keep the documentation of both as evidence that you satisfied the applicable requirements.

Can my employer enroll multiple collectors?

Yes. Organizations can purchase multiple seats through the Group Training page. Each learner must complete their own applicable training and proficiency requirements.

Will I receive completion documentation?

Completion documentation is provided according to the training plan and applicable requirements. Keep your training and mock collection proficiency records available for employers or regulatory review.

Where do I log in after enrolling?

Use the Student Login to access your training. If you need additional course access, visit the Course Extensions page.
