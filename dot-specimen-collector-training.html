Course curriculum

Built around the collection process collectors perform.

Course content is structured around current U.S. Department of Transportation rules, agency-specific requirements, and practical workplace responsibilities.

01

Part 40 foundations

Collector role, prohibited conduct, privacy, integrity, and the relationship between Part 40 and agency rules.

02

Collection site security

Preparing the site, preventing access to adulterants, privacy controls, and required supplies.

03

Federal CCF

Step-by-step completion, signatures, copy distribution, corrections, and documentation.

04

Urine collection procedure

Specimen provision, temperature checks, split specimens, sealing, packaging, and shipment.

05

Problem collections

Shy bladder, insufficient specimen, temperature outside range, direct observation, refusals, and flaws.

06

Mock collections

Five consecutive error-free proficiency demonstrations covering routine and problem collection scenarios.

Proficiency demonstrations

Five mock collections are part of the qualification pathway.

The mock collection work should demonstrate that the learner can perform the procedure correctly across routine and problem scenarios.

01
Routine collectionComplete a standard uneventful collection without procedural error.
02
Insufficient quantityFollow the required shy-bladder / insufficient-specimen workflow.
03
Temperature outside rangeRespond correctly when specimen temperature is outside the acceptable range.
04
Refusal to sign or initialHandle donor refusal involving the CCF or specimen seals.
05
Error-free completionComplete the required consecutive demonstrations without procedural error.

DOT agency coverage

Know which operating rule applies to your workforce.

49 CFR Part 40 provides common DOT drug and alcohol testing procedures. Covered employers also operate under their modal agency rule.

FAA

Aviation — 14 CFR Part 120

Drug and alcohol program requirements for covered aviation employers and safety-sensitive employees.

FMCSA

Motor Carrier — 49 CFR Part 382

Drug and alcohol testing requirements for CDL drivers and regulated motor carriers.

FRA

Railroad — 49 CFR Part 219

Drug and alcohol requirements for regulated railroad employees and employers.

FTA

Public Transit — 49 CFR Part 655

Drug and alcohol program requirements for covered public transportation employers and employees.

PHMSA

Pipeline — 49 CFR Part 199

Drug and alcohol requirements for pipeline operators and covered employees.

USCG

Maritime — 46 CFR Parts 4 & 16

Maritime drug and alcohol program requirements for covered vessel operations and personnel.

Regulatory basis: DOT §40.33 addresses basic information, qualification training, proficiency demonstrations, and refresher training for urine specimen collectors.
