DOT agency coverage

Know which operating rule applies to your workforce.

49 CFR Part 40 provides common DOT drug and alcohol testing procedures. Covered employers also operate under their modal agency rule.

FAA

Aviation. 14 CFR Part 120

Drug and alcohol program requirements for covered aviation employers and safety-sensitive employees.

FMCSA

Motor Carrier. 49 CFR Part 382

Drug and alcohol testing requirements for CDL drivers and regulated motor carriers.

FRA

Railroad. 49 CFR Part 219

Drug and alcohol requirements for regulated railroad employees and employers.

FTA

Public Transit. 49 CFR Part 655

Drug and alcohol program requirements for covered public transportation employers and employees.

PHMSA

Pipeline. 49 CFR Part 199

Drug and alcohol requirements for pipeline operators and covered employees.

USCG

Maritime. 46 CFR Parts 4 & 16

Maritime drug and alcohol program requirements for covered vessel operations and personnel.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common employee training questions.

Find information about DOT employee education, testing rules, enrollment, and course access.

Who should take DOT employee drug and alcohol training?

This course is intended for employees performing safety-sensitive duties under DOT-regulated drug and alcohol testing programs. Employers should assign education that matches the applicable agency rule and each employee's responsibilities.

What does employee drug and alcohol training cover?

The curriculum addresses prohibited drug and alcohol conduct, circumstances that can require testing, employee responsibilities, the testing process, and the consequences of violations under applicable DOT rules.

Is employee training required under every DOT agency rule?

Employee education and training requirements differ among DOT operating administrations. Employers must confirm which instruction, materials, and training duration apply to their workforce rather than assuming one course satisfies every agency's requirements.

When can a DOT-covered employee be tested?

Depending on the applicable DOT program, testing may include pre-employment, random, reasonable suspicion or reasonable cause, post-accident, return-to-duty, and follow-up testing. Not every test type applies in the same way to every agency or situation.

What happens if an employee refuses a required DOT test?

A refusal under applicable DOT rules can result in removal from safety-sensitive functions and additional return-to-duty requirements before the employee may resume covered work. Employers must follow the relevant DOT definitions and procedures.

Can an employer enroll multiple employees?

Yes. This page offers individual and multi-employee enrollment plans. You can also visit Group Training to review options for larger teams.

Will employees receive training documentation?

Course completion documentation is provided according to the selected training plan and applicable completion requirements. Employers should retain records needed for their regulated program.

Where can employees access training after enrollment?

Employees can access assigned courses through the Student Login. For additional access options, visit the Course Extensions page.
