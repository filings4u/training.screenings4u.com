Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how screenings4u handles information collected through the Learning Center and related training services.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Updated: August 23, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, company information, account identifiers, purchase records, course enrollments, progress, assessment activity, certificate records, support communications, scheduling information, and technical information such as browser or device data.

2. Payment Information

Payments are processed by Stripe or another approved processor. screenings4u receives transaction details needed to identify and fulfill your order, but payment card information is handled by the payment processor according to its own security practices.

3. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage Learning Center accounts.
  • Process orders, receipts, refunds, group-seat purchases, and course extensions.
  • Enroll learners and track training progress.
  • Issue certificates and maintain completion records.
  • Send account invitations, receipts, service notices, and support responses.
  • Protect the Learning Center and investigate misuse or fraud.
  • Improve training content and platform performance.

4. Service Providers

We may share information with service providers that support hosting, authentication, payment processing, email delivery, video delivery, scheduling, analytics, and other Learning Center operations. Providers may use information only as needed to deliver their services to us.

5. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide training services, maintain transaction and certificate records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect Learning Center information. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to or correction of certain personal information, subject to applicable law and recordkeeping requirements. Transaction, compliance, and certificate records may need to be retained.

8. Nationwide Privacy Requests

screenings4u serves learners and organizations throughout the United States. You may contact us regarding privacy questions, access or correction requests, or other qualifying privacy requests. We review and process eligible requests in accordance with applicable federal and state privacy laws.

9. Children

The Learning Center is intended for professional and employment-related training and is not directed to children under 13.

10. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to support@screenings4u.com.
