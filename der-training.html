DER responsibilities

Be ready to act when a testing decision matters.

From receiving a verified positive result to arranging a required test, the DER must understand when the employer needs to act promptly and how to document those actions.

Service agents can assist with administration, but they do not replace the employer's authorized decision-maker.

Industry requirements

Different transportation modes have different employer rules.

49 CFR Part 40 provides common procedures, but each regulated mode may impose additional program obligations.

FMCSA

Motor carriers

49 CFR Part 382 for regulated motor carrier drug and alcohol testing.

FAA

Aviation

14 CFR Part 120 for covered aviation employers.

FRA

Railroad

49 CFR Part 219 for covered railroad operations.

FTA

Public transit

49 CFR Part 655 for covered transit employers.

PHMSA

Pipeline

49 CFR Part 199 for covered pipeline operations.

USCG

Maritime

46 CFR Parts 4 and 16 for covered maritime employers.

Common questions

Answers before you enroll.

What is a Designated Employer Representative?

A DER is an employee authorized to take immediate action to remove employees from safety-sensitive duties when required and make necessary decisions in the employer's drug and alcohol testing program.

Does DOT require a universal DER certification?

No. DOT defines DER responsibilities but does not establish one universal federal DER certification. This course is training in the role, not a claim of federal certification.

Can a C/TPA act as the DER?

A service agent may support the employer, but the employer's DER responsibilities cannot generally be delegated to an external service agent. The employer must designate an authorized employee.

What topics are covered?

Part 40 procedures, required testing situations, communication with service agents, test results, removals, confidentiality, records and mode-specific requirements.

Can we enroll several staff members?

Yes. Choose an individual enrollment or one of the five, ten or twenty learner options above. Group training is also available.
