Course curriculum

Built around the training elements hazmat employees need.

Hazmat training is function-driven. The employer must identify the regulated work the employee performs and provide the training that applies to those duties.

01

General awareness / familiarization

HMR purpose, hazardous materials recognition, hazard communication, and regulatory structure.

02

Function-specific training

Requirements that apply to shipping, packaging, handling, loading, documentation, or transport duties.

03

Safety training

Emergency response information, protective measures, accident prevention, and safe handling.

04

Security awareness

Transportation-security risks, threat awareness, and methods to enhance hazmat security.

05

In-depth security

Additional training for employees with responsibilities under a required hazmat security plan.

06

Modal and driver training

Additional air, vessel, highway, rail, or motor-vehicle requirements when applicable.

Who this is for

Training matched to the role — not a one-size-fits-all course.

Hazmat employees

  • Employees who load, unload, handle, package, mark, label, document, or transport hazardous materials
  • Drivers and operators whose work affects hazmat transportation safety
  • Employees responsible for hazmat transportation safety

Hazmat employers

  • Shippers and carriers needing initial/recurrent training
  • Employers documenting training and testing under §172.704
  • Organizations needing function-specific or modal training tracks
PHMSA’s hazmat training framework requires employers to train, test, certify, and retain records. Training must be completed within the applicable initial/new-function timeframe and repeated at the required recurrent interval.
Questions

Before you enroll.

Is this training intended for DOT-regulated programs?

Yes. The course content is structured around the role and the DOT framework identified on this page. Employers remain responsible for applying the rule that governs their specific operation.

Can an employer purchase seats for multiple learners?

Yes. Choose one of the multi-learner pricing plans on this page for team enrollment.
