Keep recordkeeping responsibilities clear

Course availability, documentation, and certificate requirements vary by program. Your organization remains responsible for retaining required records and verifying applicable proficiency or qualification steps.

Available completion documentation should support—not replace—your organization’s broader training and compliance records.
