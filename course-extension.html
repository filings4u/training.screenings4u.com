COURSE EXTENSION

Add 30 days to your current course.

—

Extensions are attached to the course you already purchased. There is no course picker. Open My Courses and click Extend on that course; checkout carries that enrollment automatically and the Learning Center adds 30 days after payment.

Your progress, completed lessons, assessments and certificate history stay with the same enrollment. The updated access-through date will appear in My Courses.
