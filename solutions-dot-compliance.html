Understand what coursework does—and does not—cover

Training supports the people working within your compliance program. It does not replace employer oversight, required policies, or agency-specific obligations.

Online coursework alone does not automatically establish regulatory qualification for every task. DOT urine specimen collector qualification includes required proficiency demonstrations in addition to instruction.

Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Does online collector training replace mock collections?

No. Required proficiency demonstrations are additional to online instruction.

Are requirements the same for every transportation operation?

No. Agency-specific rules may vary by transportation mode. Verify the requirements applicable to your organization.

Can we purchase training for several program roles?

Yes. Select the relevant courses and review their separate enrollment or group package options.
