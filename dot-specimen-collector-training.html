What you will learn

Training for each step of a compliant collection.

From site preparation to completing the Federal CCF, explore the key responsibilities of a DOT urine specimen collector.

01

Part 40 fundamentals

Collector responsibilities, privacy, specimen integrity and applicable DOT procedures.

02

Site security, collection supplies, donor privacy and measures to prevent adulteration.

03

Form completion, donor and collector signatures, seals, copy distribution and corrections.

04

Specimen provision, temperature checks, split specimens, sealing and shipment.

Insufficient specimen, out-of-range temperature, directly observed collections and refusals.

06

Qualification demonstrations

Practice scenarios and documentation for the required error-free mock collections.

Practical proficiency

Online lessons are only part of collector qualification.

DOT collector qualification under 49 CFR §40.33 also requires five consecutive error-free mock collections with a qualified monitor. Review the required scenarios and the next steps for documenting proficiency.

Frequently asked questions

Collector training questions, answered.

Does the online course alone qualify me as a DOT collector?

No. Qualification also requires the proficiency demonstrations specified under 49 CFR §40.33, including five consecutive error-free mock collections. Maintain documentation of both training and proficiency.

How many mock collections are required?

DOT requires five consecutive error-free mock collections, including routine and problem collection scenarios, monitored by a qualified person.

Can someone new to collections enroll?

Yes. The collector course is intended for people learning Part 40 collection procedures as well as existing collectors reviewing those requirements.

How long do I have access?

The collector plans listed here provide 60 days of course access. Confirm the exact terms for your selected plan during enrollment.

Can my employer enroll several people?

Yes. Explore the Group Training page for multiple learners. Each collector must complete their own qualification requirements.

Where do I find my course after purchase?

Use Student Sign In to access assigned training. For more time, review Course Extensions.
