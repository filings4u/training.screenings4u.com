DOT agency coverage

Know which operating rule applies to your workforce.

49 CFR Part 40 provides common DOT drug and alcohol testing procedures. Covered employers also operate under their modal agency rule.

FAA

Aviation. 14 CFR Part 120

Drug and alcohol program requirements for covered aviation employers and safety-sensitive employees.

FMCSA

Motor Carrier. 49 CFR Part 382

Drug and alcohol testing requirements for CDL drivers and regulated motor carriers.

FRA

Railroad. 49 CFR Part 219

Drug and alcohol requirements for regulated railroad employees and employers.

FTA

Public Transit. 49 CFR Part 655

Drug and alcohol program requirements for covered public transportation employers and employees.

PHMSA

Pipeline. 49 CFR Part 199

Drug and alcohol requirements for pipeline operators and covered employees.

USCG

Maritime. 46 CFR Parts 4 & 16

Maritime drug and alcohol program requirements for covered vessel operations and personnel.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common DER training questions.

Learn about DER responsibilities, training requirements, enrollment, and course access.

What does a Designated Employer Representative do?

The DER is an employer's authorized point of contact for its DOT drug and alcohol testing program. A DER receives certain testing information and takes prompt action when required, including removing an employee from safety-sensitive duties when applicable.

Who should take DER training?

This course is intended for employer representatives who oversee DOT drug and alcohol testing, coordinate with service agents, manage required follow-up actions, or share DER responsibilities within their organization.

Is DER certification required by the DOT?

DOT regulations define the DER role, but do not establish a separate, universal DER certification credential. This course provides training on the role and its compliance responsibilities; course completion should not be described as a federal DER certification.

What topics does the DER course cover?

Topics include 49 CFR Part 40 procedures, employer and DER responsibilities, test events and results, communication with service agents, recordkeeping, and agency-specific program requirements.

Does the course address different DOT agencies?

Yes. The training discusses the Part 40 framework and differences among DOT agency programs. Employers must also follow the regulations applicable to their specific transportation operations.

Can an employer enroll multiple DERs?

Yes. The DER training plans include individual and team enrollment options. Review the available plans on this page or visit Group Training for additional organizational enrollment options.

Will I receive documentation after completing the course?

The training includes completion documentation in accordance with the selected plan and its completion requirements. Keep your training records with your organization's compliance documentation.

Where do I access my DER course after enrolling?

Sign in through the Student Login to access assigned training. For additional access options, see the Course Extensions page.
