WORKFORCE TRAINING BY SCREENINGS4U

Accessibility Statement

Our accessibility commitments, assistance process and approach to online and monitored training.

Effective November 10, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026

1. Accessibility Commitment

Workforce Training by screenings4u seeks to make its training website, purchase process and LMS usable to learners with a range of abilities. We work toward improvement but do not claim a formal accessibility certification or completed third-party conformance assessment.

2. Website and Course Features

We aim to provide understandable content, keyboard-accessible controls, form labels, appropriate text contrast, and layouts that work on desktop and mobile. Videos, downloadable materials, assessments and third-party components may have additional accessibility requirements.

3. Requesting Assistance

If you encounter a barrier, contact our team with the affected page or course, the issue and a convenient response method. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive medical information in a general support form.

4. Practical and Monitored Sessions

Some in-person or monitored qualification activities involve legally prescribed standards, equipment or verification. We will discuss reasonable arrangements while ensuring that required qualifications are not misrepresented.

5. Third-Party Services

Payment services, learning media and other providers may operate independent interfaces. Report access issues so we can review available alternatives or escalate the concern.

6. Feedback and Response

Submit accessibility feedback to support@screenings4u.com or https://training.screenings4u.com/contact.html. We will investigate and explain available options, recognizing that some issues may require coordination with a service provider.

7. Updates

This statement may be revised as the website, LMS, courses and accessibility practices change.
