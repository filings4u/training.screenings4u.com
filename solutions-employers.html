Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Can we enroll multiple employees?

Yes. Group enrollment options are available through dedicated course-specific package pages. Review the relevant page for current options and pricing.

Can everyone take the same course?

Choose training according to actual responsibilities. Employees, supervisors, DERs, collectors, and HazMat personnel may need different coursework.

Does online training replace our compliance program?

No. Employers remain responsible for applicable requirements, workplace policies, and program decisions.
