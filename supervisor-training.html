Supervisor decision process

Base decisions on observable facts, not guesses.

Under applicable DOT rules, reasonable suspicion determinations rely on specific, contemporaneous observations. This course helps supervisors understand what to record, whom to contact and how to proceed.

DOT agency requirements

Follow the rules specific to your operation.

Supervisor training and reasonable suspicion procedures vary among DOT operating modes.

FMCSA

Motor carriers

49 CFR Part 382 includes reasonable suspicion supervisor training obligations.

FAA

Aviation

14 CFR Part 120 provides training and testing requirements for covered aviation programs.

FRA

Railroads

49 CFR Part 219 governs regulated railroad drug and alcohol testing.

FTA

Public transit

49 CFR Part 655 covers transit reasonable suspicion programs.

PHMSA

Pipeline

49 CFR Part 199 governs covered pipeline drug and alcohol testing.

USCG

Maritime

46 CFR Parts 4 and 16 govern covered maritime programs.

Common questions

Supervisor training, explained.

Who should take DOT supervisor training?

Supervisors who may be required to observe, document and initiate reasonable suspicion testing for DOT-covered employees.

How many training hours are required?

For FMCSA-covered supervisors, 49 CFR §382.603 requires at least 60 minutes on alcohol misuse indicators and 60 minutes on controlled substance use indicators. Requirements differ for other agencies.

Can I make a determination based on a hunch?

No. Applicable reasonable suspicion decisions must be grounded in specific, contemporaneous and articulable observations, not unsupported guesses.

Does this cover drug and alcohol indicators?

Yes. The training addresses indicators, documentation and appropriate referral procedures for both.
