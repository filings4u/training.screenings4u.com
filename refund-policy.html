Refund Policy

This policy applies to Learning Center course purchases, group training products, course extensions, and training supplies.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Updated: August 23, 2026

1. Online Training Courses and Digital Products

All online training course purchases are final and non-refundable.

screenings4u Learning Center provides digital products and online educational services, including self-paced courses, instructional videos, digital training materials, assessments, mock collection activities, downloadable resources, certificates, and other electronically delivered content.

Because these products are digital and course access may be provided immediately after purchase, we do not offer refunds, cancellations, credits, or exchanges for online training courses once a purchase has been completed, regardless of whether the learner has started, partially completed, or completed the course.

This policy applies even if the learner does not begin or complete the course; changes their mind after purchasing; no longer needs the training; purchases the wrong course; fails to complete the course within the applicable access period; does not successfully complete required quizzes, assessments, mock collections, or other course requirements; downloads or accesses digital course materials; receives employer reimbursement or later determines that an employer will not reimburse the purchase; or requests a refund after completing or substantially completing the training.

Course access, digital materials, instructional content, and other intellectual property cannot be returned in the same manner as physical merchandise. This no-refund policy also protects against the use, completion, copying, or consumption of digital training content followed by a request to reverse the purchase.

Purchasers are responsible for reviewing the course description, price, eligibility requirements, included materials, access period, technology requirements, and any certification or completion requirements before completing their purchase.

If you experience a technical problem that prevents you from accessing a course you purchased, contact screenings4u Learning Center Support. We will make reasonable efforts to restore access or correct the technical issue. A technical support request does not automatically qualify a purchase for a refund.

Duplicate charges, billing errors, or other payment-processing errors should be reported to Support for review. Correcting a verified billing error is not considered a refund of a properly completed course purchase.

2. Group Training

Unused group-seat purchases may be reviewed for cancellation before seats are assigned or training access is used. Once a seat has been assigned or used, the value associated with that seat is non-refundable. Custom live-training or organization-specific services may have separate cancellation terms communicated at scheduling.

3. Course Extensions

Course extensions are applied automatically to the associated enrollment after successful payment. Once the extension has been applied, the extension purchase is non-refundable except where required by law or when screenings4u confirms a processing error.

4. Training Supplies

Unshipped physical training-supply orders may be eligible for cancellation. Shipped supplies are generally non-refundable unless damaged, defective, or incorrect. Digital downloads included with a supply purchase are non-refundable once delivered.

5. Duplicate or Incorrect Charges

If you believe you were charged twice or charged an incorrect amount, contact us promptly. We will review the transaction and correct confirmed billing errors.

6. How to Request a Refund

Email support@screenings4u.com with the purchaser name, email address, order number, and reason for the request.
